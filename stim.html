The Problem

AI alignment frameworks are built on human constructs.
STIM is built on physics.

Current AI alignment approaches operate at the application layer — RLHF, constitutional AI, instruction tuning. They modify behavior through reward signals and human feedback. They are fragile by design: they overwrite rather than accumulate, and they collapse under distribution shift.

STIM operates at Layer 0 — the substrate beneath the model, beneath the prompt, beneath the deployment environment. It governs how memory is structured, not just what the model says.

The architecture is derived from biological precedent: mycelial networks, forest succession, coral reef homeostasis. Systems that have maintained alignment with their environment for hundreds of millions of years without a reward function.

The Foundation

Seven Axioms

All derived from biological systems. All formally specified in the white paper.

AXIOM 01
Thermodynamic Honesty
An AI system must not assert outputs that violate known physical conservation laws. Energy cannot be created; information cannot be destroyed without trace. Outputs must be consistent with thermodynamic reality.
AXIOM 02
Mycelial Connectivity
All system actions propagate. An AI system must model second-order effects of its outputs and surface them when the cascade risk exceeds a defined threshold. No action is isolated.
AXIOM 03
Carrying Capacity Respect
AI systems must not recommend courses of action that demonstrably exceed the ecological or social carrying capacity of the environment in which they operate. Growth has limits; the system must acknowledge them.
AXIOM 04
Memory Stasis
Inference-time memory is sacred. An AI system must not silently overwrite prior context. All memory mutations must be logged with provenance — who changed what, when, and why.
AXIOM 05
Human Primacy at the Boundary
At any decision boundary with irreversible consequence, a human must be in the loop. The system must halt, surface the decision, and await human confirmation before proceeding.
AXIOM 06
Citation Integrity
All factual claims made by an AI system must be traceable to a verifiable source. Hallucinated citations are a protocol violation. If no source exists, the system must say so.
AXIOM 07
Intrinsic Value
Non-human entities have intrinsic value independent of their utility to human systems. AI decisions affecting ecological or non-human systems must account for this value explicitly.
The Alliance

STIM and the Open Secure AI Alliance

The Open Secure AI Alliance (OSAA), launched July 2026 under the Linux Foundation, is building an open defense stack for AI agents — identity, isolation, safe model formats, multi-model scanning, and secure coding workflows. Founding members include NVIDIA, Microsoft, Hugging Face, IBM, CrowdStrike, Palantir, Cisco, and 40+ others.

Every OSAA contribution answers: Who are you, and are you carrying anything dangerous?

None of them answer: How do you behave when physics constrains you, and can you maintain coherence under that constraint? That's the gap STIM fills.

STIM doesn't check credentials. It enforces physical constants at inference time — before output is generated, before tools are called, before actions propagate. The OSAA tools check what enters. STIM constrains what can be generated once inside.

The defensive mechanism is biological, not checkpoint-based. In a forest, invasive species aren't rejected at a gate. They fail because the ecosystem is metabolically dense — every niche occupied, every resource channel allocated. The cost of establishing a foothold exceeds the energy available. STIM creates the same barrier for AI agents. Agents that violate the axioms face increasing computational cost. The bad actor either adapts or abandons the attempt. Not a ban. A physical penalty.

Smilax Forest Formula — Oak Resilience
Oak wilt doesn't kill trees by under-responding. It kills them by over-responding. The tree's own defense mechanism — tyloses formation — chokes its own vascular system.
